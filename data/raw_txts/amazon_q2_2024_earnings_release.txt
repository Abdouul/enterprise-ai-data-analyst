AMAZON.COM, INC.
Q2 2024 EARNINGS RELEASE AND CFO COMMENTARY
For the Three Months Ended June 30, 2024

================================================================================
FINANCIAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Amazon reported second-quarter 2024 results that significantly exceeded analyst
expectations, driven by accelerating Amazon Web Services (AWS) revenue growth
and continued North America segment margin expansion. Total net sales reached
$148.0 billion, up 10% year-over-year (11% excluding foreign exchange impact).

CFO Brian Olsavsky stated: "We're seeing strong demand signals across the
business. AWS growth has reaccelerated, advertising continues to outperform,
and we're making meaningful progress on cost-to-serve reductions within our
fulfillment network."

================================================================================
SEGMENT RESULTS (Q2 2024)
================================================================================

NORTH AMERICA SEGMENT:
  Net Sales:                     $90.0B    (+9% YoY)
  Operating Income:               $5.1B    (5.7% operating margin)
    vs. prior year operating margin of 3.9% — a 180bps improvement

  Drivers of margin expansion:
    - Regionalization of fulfillment network reduced average cost-to-serve by
      approximately $0.45 per unit delivered
    - Reduced air freight reliance following post-COVID inventory normalization
    - Lower fuel costs vs. peak 2022 levels
    - Advertising revenue attached to product listings (high-margin)

INTERNATIONAL SEGMENT:
  Net Sales:                     $31.7B   (+10% YoY, +13% ex-FX)
  Operating Income:               $0.3B    (first-ever positive operating income
                                           for two consecutive quarters)

  Key markets:
    - UK: Consistently profitable, growing in logistics and Prime Video
    - Germany: Returned to profitability in Q1 2024 after labor cost pressures
    - Japan: Stable profitability; subscription revenue growing
    - Emerging markets (India, Brazil, Middle East): Still loss-making but
      narrowing losses as infrastructure investment matures

AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AWS):
  Net Sales:                     $26.3B   (+19% YoY)
  Operating Income:               $9.3B   (35.5% operating margin)

  AWS is now growing at its fastest pace since Q3 2022, driven by:
    1. Enterprise AI workloads: Companies migrating from exploratory AI pilots
       to production-scale deployments, requiring GPU compute and managed
       AI services (Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker).
    2. Optimization cycle completion: The "optimization headwind" of 2022-2023,
       when enterprises aggressively right-sized cloud spend, has substantially
       unwound. Committed spend drawdowns are declining.
    3. New product attach: AWS has expanded from ~230 services to over 240,
       with new AI-native services including Bedrock (generative AI API), Q
       (AI coding assistant), and Trainium/Inferentia custom chips.

================================================================================
AWS AI PLATFORM: AMAZON BEDROCK
================================================================================

Amazon Bedrock is AWS's fully managed generative AI service, allowing enterprises
to build AI applications using foundation models from Amazon, Anthropic, Meta,
Mistral, Cohere, and others — without managing underlying infrastructure.

Key Bedrock capabilities:
  - Model access: Amazon Titan, Claude 3 (Anthropic), Llama 3 (Meta), Command
    (Cohere), Mistral models
  - Knowledge Bases: Native RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with vector
    store integration (OpenSearch Serverless, Aurora PostgreSQL, Pinecone)
  - Agents: Multi-step autonomous agents that can call APIs, query databases,
    and execute business logic
  - Guardrails: Content filtering, PII detection, topic blocking
  - Model Evaluation: Automated benchmarking of fine-tuned vs. base models

Bedrock crossed 100,000 active customers in Q2 2024, though average revenue
per customer remains modest as organizations are in early production phases.

AWS custom silicon strategy:
  - Trainium2 (training): Second-generation ML training chip offering 4x
    throughput improvement over Trainium1; used by Amazon internally and
    offered to select enterprise customers
  - Inferentia2 (inference): Optimized for low-latency, high-throughput
    inference at scale; priced 40-50% below equivalent NVIDIA-based instances
  - Graviton4 (general compute): ARM-based CPU for web, database, and analytics
    workloads; offers 30% better price-performance vs. x86 alternatives

================================================================================
ADVERTISING BUSINESS
================================================================================

Amazon Advertising is now the company's highest-margin revenue stream on a
standalone basis, generating approximately $14.7B in Q2 2024, up 19% YoY.

The advertising product suite includes:
  - Sponsored Products / Brands / Display: Search and browse ads on Amazon.com
  - Streaming TV ads: DSP targeting against Prime Video and Freevee inventory
    (Prime Video ads launched January 2024, representing a significant new
    inventory source)
  - Amazon DSP: Programmatic display and video advertising across the web

Prime Video Ad-Supported Tier: Amazon's decision to insert advertising into
Prime Video (previously ad-free) for all subscribers unless they pay $2.99/month
to opt out is expected to generate $3-5 billion in incremental annual ad revenue
at scale. Initial CPMs (cost per thousand impressions) for Prime Video ads are
reported at $30-35, premium to most streaming competitors, due to Amazon's
first-party purchase data enabling superior audience targeting.

================================================================================
FULFILLMENT NETWORK AND LOGISTICS
================================================================================

SAME-DAY DELIVERY EXPANSION:
  Amazon operates 55 same-day delivery facilities in the US, enabling delivery
  within hours for millions of Prime members in major metropolitan areas. The
  company plans to double this count by end of 2025, supporting its strategy
  to offer same-day delivery to 50% of the US population.

LAST-MILE DELIVERY:
  Amazon Logistics (AMZL) now delivers approximately 72% of Amazon's US volume,
  with the remainder handled by UPS, USPS, and FedEx. The company's delivery
  service partner (DSP) network comprises over 3,000 small businesses employing
  200,000+ drivers. This in-house logistics network is now serving third parties
  through Amazon Shipping, directly competing with UPS and FedEx.

ROBOTICS:
  Amazon has deployed over 750,000 robots across its fulfillment network,
  including Proteus (autonomous mobile robot), Cardinal (AI-powered sortation
  arm), and Sparrow (item-picking robot). These systems are improving throughput
  and reducing repetitive-strain injuries but have also contributed to workforce
  reduction discussions.

================================================================================
RISKS AND CHALLENGES
================================================================================

1. Labor relations: Amazon faces ongoing unionization efforts following the
   Amazon Labor Union's landmark 2022 victory at JFK8. A second union drive
   at the Coventry, UK warehouse resulted in a union victory in 2024.
2. Antitrust: FTC lawsuit (filed September 2023) alleges Amazon maintains an
   illegal monopoly over online marketplace services, seeking potential
   structural remedies including divestiture of certain marketplace or logistics
   assets.
3. AWS competition: Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are investing aggressively
   in AI infrastructure and competing on enterprise relationships.
4. International profitability: Emerging market operations require continued
   investment with uncertain payback timelines.
5. AI infrastructure capital: $30B+ annual capex commitment to AI data centers
   requires sustained demand to generate acceptable returns on investment.
