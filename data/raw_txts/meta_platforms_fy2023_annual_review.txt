THE YEAR OF EFFICIENCY: STRATEGIC OVERVIEW
================================================================================

2023 was Meta's self-declared "Year of Efficiency." Following a period of
aggressive headcount and cost expansion in 2021-2022 — driven by the ambition
to build the Metaverse and accelerated by over-hiring during the COVID surge —
the company executed one of the most significant corporate restructurings in
Silicon Valley history:

  Total headcount reduced by approximately 21,000 positions (21% of workforce)
  across three rounds of layoffs in 2022-2023
  Operating expenses reduced by ~$10 billion from peak
  Return to strong free cash flow generation: $43.0 billion in FY2023

The operational discipline paid off dramatically. Meta stock, which had fallen
76% from its 2021 peak by the end of 2022, recovered over 200% in 2023, making
it the best-performing stock in the S&P 500 for the year.

ADVERTISING BUSINESS: AI-DRIVEN PERFORMANCE RECOVERY
================================================================================

Meta's advertising business powered back to growth after a difficult 2022,
driven by three primary factors:

1. APPLE ATT MITIGATION:
   Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, introduced in 2021,
   prevented Meta from tracking iPhone users across apps without explicit opt-in.
   Initially devastating (~$10B annual revenue impact estimate), Meta has
   substantially mitigated ATT through:
   - Conversion API (CAPI): Server-to-server event transmission bypassing
     ATT restrictions
   - Modeled conversion signals: AI/ML models that estimate conversion events
     that can no longer be directly tracked
   - On-device machine learning: Aggregated measurement that preserves privacy
     while providing sufficient attribution signal for ad optimization

2. ADVANTAGE+ AUTOMATED CAMPAIGNS:
   Meta's AI-powered campaign automation suite (Advantage+) uses machine
   learning to automatically optimize audience targeting, ad creative selection,
   bidding, and budget allocation. Advantage+ campaigns now account for over
   40% of Meta's advertising volume, and advertisers using Advantage+
   report 32% better cost-per-acquisition on average vs. manually managed campaigns.
   
   This represents a fundamental shift: advertisers no longer need to define
   precise audiences; they provide creative assets and conversion objectives,
   and Meta's AI handles the rest.

3. REELS MONETIZATION:
   Short-form video (Reels) was initially a drag on revenue as it cannibalized
   higher-CPM Feed and Stories inventory. By 2023, Reels monetization efficiency
   reached parity with other formats, and Reels is now contributing positively
   to revenue growth rather than diluting it.

REALITY LABS: THE METAVERSE BET
================================================================================

Reality Labs, Meta's virtual and augmented reality division, reported:
  FY2023 Revenue:       $1.9B    (Quest headset sales and content)
  FY2023 Operating Loss: ($16.1B)  (accumulated losses exceed $40B since 2020)

QUEST 3:
  Launched October 2023 at $499. The Quest 3 features mixed reality (color
  passthrough cameras for seeing the real world with digital overlays), improved
  resolution, and faster processing. Launch reception was positive, with sell-
  through exceeding Quest 2's launch by approximately 30%.

RAY-BAN META SMART GLASSES:
  In collaboration with EssilorLuxottica, Meta launched second-generation
  Ray-Ban Meta glasses featuring an open-ear speaker, camera, and Meta AI
  voice assistant integration. Over 1 million units sold by year-end, making
  them the most commercially successful smart glasses product in history.
  The glasses do not feature AR overlays (no display), which limits functionality
  but dramatically reduces battery size and cost.

ORION (AR GLASSES — PROJECT):
  Meta is reportedly developing true AR glasses (Project Orion) with
  holographic waveguide displays. Internal prototypes weigh approximately
  100g and offer a 70-degree field of view. Mass-market launch is expected
  no earlier than 2027-2028, pending further miniaturization of display,
  compute, and battery technology.

LLAMA AND META AI STRATEGY
================================================================================

Meta's open-source AI strategy, anchored by the Llama series of large language
models, has become a major strategic asset:

  Llama 2: Released July 2023 under a commercial-friendly license.
  Downloaded over 30 million times in six months, becoming the dominant
  open-source foundation model and a major competitive threat to OpenAI's
  GPT-4 API.

  Meta AI Assistant: Integrated into all Family of Apps (Facebook, Instagram,
  WhatsApp, Messenger) and the Quest headset as a general-purpose AI assistant.
  Users can ask questions, generate images (using Meta's Emu diffusion model),
  and receive recommendations.

  Code Llama: Specialized code generation model. Llama Guard: Safety
  classifier for detecting harmful content.

Strategy rationale for open-source AI:
  - Reduces cost: The broader ecosystem improves the models and discovers
    training techniques that Meta benefits from
  - Talent: Open-source leadership attracts top AI researchers
  - Regulatory: Open-source positioning complicates EU AI Act compliance
    requirements for closed frontier models
  - Commoditization: By making strong foundation models freely available,
    Meta reduces the competitive moat of OpenAI and Anthropic
