TESLA, INC.
Q4 AND FULL YEAR 2023 UPDATE
For the Quarter and Year Ended December 31, 2023

================================================================================
SHAREHOLDER LETTER SUMMARY
================================================================================

Tesla's mission — accelerating the world's transition to sustainable energy —
drove record deliveries in 2023, with 1.81 million vehicles delivered globally,
a 38% increase versus 2022. However, the year was marked by aggressive price
reductions that weighed significantly on margins, as the company prioritized
volume and market share over near-term profitability.

The energy storage business reached an inflection point. Megapack deployments
hit a record 14.7 GWh in 2023, generating $6.0 billion in Energy Generation
and Storage segment revenue. This segment's economics are becoming increasingly
important to Tesla's long-term financial profile.

================================================================================
PRICING STRATEGY AND MARGIN PRESSURE
================================================================================

Tesla initiated a series of price cuts beginning in January 2023, reducing
average selling prices by 15-25% across its lineup in the United States and
other key markets. The rationale was threefold:

1. DEMAND STIMULATION: Rising interest rates materially increased monthly
   payment burdens for financed buyers. A $5,000 reduction in vehicle price
   offsets approximately $80-90/month in payment at prevailing rates.

2. MARKET SHARE DEFENSE: Competition from BYD, Volkswagen ID series, Hyundai/Kia,
   and GM's Chevrolet Equinox EV required Tesla to maintain price competitiveness.

3. IRA ELIGIBILITY: Pricing vehicles below the Inflation Reduction Act thresholds
   ($55,000 for cars, $80,000 for SUVs/trucks) to maximize consumer tax credit
   eligibility and stimulate US demand.

Impact: Automotive gross margin declined from approximately 29% in Q1 2022 to
17.6% by Q4 2023, a contraction of over 1,000 basis points. Management guided
toward prioritizing volume in the near term, with margin recovery expected as
the cost reduction roadmap (next-generation vehicle platform, 48V architecture,
die-cast manufacturing) is implemented.

================================================================================
FULL SELF-DRIVING (FSD) AND AI STRATEGY
================================================================================

Tesla's vision-only Full Self-Driving system crossed 1 billion miles driven
cumulatively in 2023. The company released FSD v12, a complete rewrite using
end-to-end neural networks trained on real-world video data, abandoning the
previous hand-coded rules-based approach.

FSD Monetization:
  - FSD package price (US): $12,000 one-time or $199/month subscription
  - FSD attach rate at purchase: estimated 12-15%
  - Total FSD deferred revenue balance: approximately $3.2 billion
    (recognized over the software update life of each vehicle)

Dojo Supercomputer: Tesla's proprietary AI training cluster, using internally
designed D1 chips, reached over 1 exaflop of processing capacity. The system
is designed exclusively for training Tesla's neural networks on video data from
the fleet's cameras. Elon Musk has indicated Dojo capacity may be sold to third
parties, positioning Tesla as an AI infrastructure company.

Robotaxi Platform: Tesla announced plans to launch a robotaxi service using
a purpose-built vehicle (no steering wheel, no pedals) by 2025. The success
of this plan is entirely dependent on regulatory approval for autonomous driving
in key jurisdictions — a timeline that has repeatedly slipped.

================================================================================
ENERGY BUSINESS MOMENTUM
================================================================================

The Energy Generation and Storage segment is increasingly material:

  Megapack (grid-scale storage) deployments:  14.7 GWh  (+125% YoY)
  Powerwall (home storage) deployments:       ~600,000 units
  Solar Roof & Solar Panels:                   ~96 MW installed

Megapack gross margin reached approximately 24% in Q4 2023, exceeding
Automotive gross margin for the first time — a significant structural milestone.
Tesla's Lathrop, CA Megafactory produces approximately 40 GWh/year of Megapack
capacity; a second factory in Shanghai is under development.

================================================================================
KEY RISKS
================================================================================

1. Chinese EV competition: BYD surpassed Tesla in global EV deliveries in
   Q4 2023. BYD's vertically integrated battery and vehicle manufacturing gives
   it a structural cost advantage in China and emerging markets.
2. Elon Musk key-person risk: Musk's involvement with X (Twitter), SpaceX,
   xAI, and political activities raises governance concerns and management
   bandwidth questions.
3. Autonomous driving regulatory risk: FSD approval timelines are uncertain
   in the US, EU, and China.
4. Margin recovery timeline: Cost reduction programs may take 18-24 months
   to materially recover automotive margins.
5. Cybertruck ramp: Production challenges and mixed consumer reception.
6. Labor relations: The UAW organizing campaign at Giga Texas represents a
   new labor risk for Tesla, which has historically operated as a non-union shop.
